# Quickstart: Validating Email Notifications

1. Walk one request through submit, release, three approvals: each expected email arrives to the right recipients; each administrator receives their own copy.
2. Reject and return: the Creator email includes the comments.
3. Resubmit: administrators notified.
4. Final approval: administrators and Creator emails.
5. Make one person manager and Plant Manager of a request: one consolidated email listing both actions.
6. Force the mail connector to fail: retries happen, failure recorded, administrators alerted, workflow action still completed.
7. Blank the email of a recipient or deactivate them: no send, reason recorded, administrators alerted.
8. Pending beyond N days: reminder sent.
9. Delivery within 5 minutes; every outcome appears in the notification log.
